How to play

  • Move by dragging anywhere on the left half of the screen. Push the stick further to run.
  • Steal a car: stand next to one and tap ENTER. If someone is driving, they're getting out.
  • Drive: point the stick where you want to go. Hold BRAKE to stop, keep holding to reverse. Brake while turning to slide.
  • Jobs: a payphone rings somewhere. Follow the green arrow and walk up to it. Each job done raises your cash multiplier.
  • Heat: crimes seen by cops raise the siren lights. Stay out of sight to cool off, grab a bribe badge, or drive onto a Paint & Go pad for a $500 respray.
  • Crates hold weapons, health, armor and cash. SWAP cycles weapons.
  • Keyboard: WASD to move or drive, Space fire/brake, E enter/exit, Q swap, H horn, R radio, P pause.
